I'm evaluating whether a product idea has already been built by someone
else. Please research and report back — I don't need code, just findings.

**The idea:** A neighbourhood book-sharing app/website, initially for one
community (Modiin, Israel), aimed at English-speaking residents ("Anglos")
who find English-language books expensive and hard to source locally.
Mechanic: a user photographs their bookshelves, an AI reads the spines and
builds a digital catalogue (title, author, genre, cover), the user marks
which books they're willing to lend, and neighbours can search everyone's
libraries and request to borrow. Actual lending logistics (handoff, due
dates) happen off-platform, e.g. via WhatsApp — the product is discovery
and cataloguing, not a full lending-management system.

**What I already know exists globally** (don't waste time re-discovering
these, but flag if any of them are already active *in Israel* specifically):
LibraryThing, Libib, BookMooch, Little Free Library's map/app, and various
"scan your shelf" cataloguing apps (e.g. apps built around barcode/ISBN
scanning rather than AI spine-reading).

1. Any Israeli startup, nonprofit, or community project doing this same
   thing — English-book-specific or general — anywhere in Israel, not just
   Modiin.

   informally at scale: Janglo (Jerusalem-area Anglo classifieds), ESRA,
   Nefesh B'Nefesh community resources, city-specific Facebook groups (e.g.

   book-exchange sub-feature already, versus just ad hoc posts.

   general Israeli market (not just English books) that I should be aware of

4. Any effort by Israeli public libraries or the National Library of Israel

   anything)?
